LIMITS
The live Cloudflare account, bindings, secrets and remote hosting were not
accessible for deployment. Complete START-HERE.txt before playing on the live
sites. Cross-device latency and the approximately 40-minute human play duration
still need a real two-person session; 40 minutes is a pacing estimate.
